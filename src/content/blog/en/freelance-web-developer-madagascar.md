More and more companies work with developers based in Madagascar: **solid skills, in French and English, at a more accessible rate**. I am a freelance web developer in Antananarivo; here is how a collaboration works in practice.

## Time zone

Madagascar is on **UTC+3**: one or two hours ahead of Western Europe and close to the Middle East and East Africa. Working days overlap almost completely, and you often find progress on yesterday’s feedback when you start your day.

## Language

French is an official language in Madagascar, and I also work in English with international clients. Written and spoken communication is straightforward.

## How a remote project runs

1. **A video call** to understand your needs.
2. **A fixed-price quote** and a mock-up of the key screens.
3. **A staging link** updated as development progresses, so you can comment directly.
4. **Regular check-ins** by video or message, at your pace.
5. **Launch**, then maintenance if you want it.

The code lives on GitHub, **in your account**. You stay in control at every step.

## Payment

Usually by **international transfer or a platform** (Wise, Payoneer, Upwork), with a deposit at the start and milestone payments. A signed quote fixes scope, price and timeline.

## Checking quality

Look at **live projects** rather than mock-ups, the **technical choices** (modern tools, performance, SEO) and how the developer **communicates** from the first exchange.
