It is the first question people ask me, and the honest answer is: **it depends on what the site has to do**. That does not help anyone plan a budget, so here is what actually drives the price, with orders of magnitude.

## What drives the price

A custom website is mostly **working time**. Four things move it:

1. **Pages and templates.** Ten pages built on three templates cost less than ten unique pages.
2. **Features.** A contact form takes an hour. A client area, online booking or payments take days.
3. **Design.** Adapting an existing brand is faster than creating one from scratch.
4. **Content.** Ready-made text and photos save a lot of time. Writing or producing them is separate work.

## Typical ranges (European market)

These are **indicative** figures commonly seen with freelancers and agencies.

| Project type | Freelancer | Agency |
|---|---|---|
| Showcase site (5–10 pages) | €1,500 – €5,000 | €5,000 – €15,000 |
| E-commerce site | €4,000 – €15,000 | €10,000 – €40,000 |
| Business web app | €8,000 – €30,000 | €20,000 – €80,000 |
| SaaS (first version) | €15,000+ | €40,000+ |

The gap is not necessarily about quality: agencies also bill for coordination. With a freelancer you talk directly to the person writing the code.

## Costs people forget

- **Domain name**: about €10–20 per year.
- **Hosting**: free to a few dozen euros a month for a modern showcase site; more for an app with a database.
- **Maintenance**: updates, backups, small changes, often as a monthly plan.
- **Third-party tools**: emailing, payments, CRM or maps may have their own subscriptions.

## Why a “cheap” site often costs more

A site rushed onto a generic theme looks cheap until it is **slow** (Google favours fast sites), **depends on dozens of plugins** that conflict over time, and becomes **hard to change**. A custom site only loads what it needs: faster, safer and easier to grow.

## How to get an accurate quote

Prepare your **main goal**, a rough **list of pages**, **two or three sites you like** and why, plus your **deadline** and budget. A good provider can then give you a **fixed-price quote**, detailed page by page.

## In short

For a custom showcase site built by a freelancer, expect **a few thousand euros**, more as soon as you add online sales, a client area or business logic. What matters is that the site **brings in more than it costs**.
